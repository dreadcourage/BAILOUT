BAILOUT - 2 MINUTE PITCH

Meetings have calendar invitations, agendas, and minutes. What they do not have is an eject button.

We have all been in the same situation: the agenda ended twenty minutes ago, everyone is repeating themselves, and leaving honestly somehow feels ruder than wasting another hour.

Fake-call apps already exist, but they only solve the sound. You still have to invent a caller, a believable reason, and an entire conversation while everyone is watching you.

So we built BAILOUT: the Strategic Meeting Extraction System.

Choose who is calling, choose an extraction scenario, and set a believable delay. BAILOUT calculates an extraction-confidence score and prepares a complete cue-card script.

Then arm the system - or press the Escape key at any moment for an immediate incoming call.

[PRESS ESC AND ANSWER THE CALL]

The phone rings and vibrates like a real call. Once answered, BAILOUT's audio coach speaks each line into your ear, waits for you to repeat it, and advances automatically - including the final exit line. It also logs the mission, because apparently escaping a meeting now requires enterprise analytics.

The whole MVP runs in one HTML file. No account, no installation, and no setup.

BAILOUT is useful enough to download, ridiculous enough to share, and simple enough to work live.
