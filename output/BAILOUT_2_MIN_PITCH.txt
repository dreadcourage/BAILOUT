FAST Q&A

What makes BAILOUT different from other fake-call apps?
They create a ring. BAILOUT creates the complete exit: caller, scenario, timing, confidence score, realistic call, hands-free audio coaching, and mission log.

Where is the AI?
For the MVP, we focused on making the core escape loop reliable with curated scenarios and local audio. The next step is AI-personalized callers, excuses, and follow-up lines.

Who would use this?
Students, workers, and anyone trapped in a meeting that should have been an email.

Is this just lying?
